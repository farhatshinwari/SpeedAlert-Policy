SpeedAlert Privacy Policy

Developer: IRYSCO LABS
Last updated: 25 November 2026
Contact: FARHATSHINWARI@GMAIL.COM

Free and Pro in one app

GPS speed monitoring, speed warnings, existing settings and route-learning features remain Free. An optional Google Play lifetime purchase unlocks Pro features and removes advertisements. There is no app-owned account system. Google Play, RevenueCat and file/advertising providers operate under their own policies.

Location and background monitoring

With permission, SpeedAlert uses precise location to estimate speed and provide warnings. If enabled, background monitoring accesses location while the app is closed or not in use, including with the screen locked. An ongoing notification identifies the active service. Optional All-the-time permission supports resuming a previously enabled process after reboot, subject to Android restrictions. You can disable monitoring or revoke permissions. The separate advertisements screen requests foreground GPS to restrict ads/consent during movement; uncertain/missing GPS blocks ads. Free/Pro information, Buy and Restore require monitoring disabled but do not check GPS or request location permission. Use commerce tools only while parked.

Locally saved routes and trips

Learn route stores named route sections and your selected limit privately on this device. Routes can be edited or deleted individually or all together in Settings. Pro trip history is off by default and requires explicit opt-in. When enabled, it stores sampled coordinates, timestamps, adjusted speeds, limits, observed distance and warnings locally using existing monitoring samples. You can disable recording and delete all trip history from ordinary Settings, even without Pro. Route and trip databases are excluded from backup/device transfer. No route/trip records are sent to the RevenueCat or attached to advertisement requests.

Exports and audio files

Pro users may export/import named routes, limits and precise GPS sections as SpeedAlert JSON, and export trip data as CSV or GPX to a destination they choose through Android's document picker. A selected cloud file provider may upload/store the exported file under its own policy. Exported files are outside the app's private database; deleting local history does not delete exported copies. Selected alarm audio remains local; the app keeps Android's file access grant to play it, replaces/releases old access where applicable, and does not upload the sound.

Google Play purchases and RevenueCat

Google Play processes payments; SpeedAlert does not receive full card details. RevenueCat manages purchase verification, acknowledgment, entitlement status and restoration. Its SDK sends purchase/store information, an anonymous app-user identifier and relevant device/network metadata over HTTPS to RevenueCat. The publisher can access purchase/entitlement records in RevenueCat for support. No route/trip GPS records are sent to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection, SDK diagnostics and automatic in-app messages are disabled in this integration. Review RevenueCat's privacy policy and current SDK disclosures.

Only accepted server-verified lifetime entitlements unlock Pro. An authenticated snapshot protected by Android Keystore is stored privately for offline access. Online checks reconcile through RevenueCat. Pending/cancelled purchases do not grant access; refunds/revocations require RevenueCat updates and successful online reconciliation. Offline devices cannot immediately learn of a refund. Reinstalling/clearing data removes local access; Restore Purchases can recover valid access using the purchasing Play account and RevenueCat's restore/transfer policy.

Advertising and consent

The separate GPS-checked advertisements screen may display Google AdMob banners for Free users. Verified Pro users do not receive banner requests/displays. The driving dashboard, ordinary Settings and Free/Pro purchase screen have no banner requests/displays. Advertisements and ad consent require the parked GPS check and are removed/hidden on loss of that state. Buy/Restore use the stopped-monitor check without GPS, as described above. No interstitial or rewarded ads are included.

Google's advertising SDK may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and other data described by Google. The app does not add GPS route/trip coordinates to ad requests. Applicable consent/privacy choices are requested through Google's User Messaging Platform before ad requests. Ad privacy choices are accessible in parked tools when required. Review Google's Privacy Policy and Google partner-site information. Development builds use Google's test advertisement IDs. Store Data safety declarations must reflect the configured SDK and production service.

Permissions, deletion and control

Location supports speed measurement and the parked interlock. Notifications/vibration support monitoring and alarms. A timed CPU wake lock supports screen-off operation while enabled; boot reception supports optional restart. Internet/network-state permissions support Play, verification and advertising/consent. Advertising-ID/advertising-service permissions may be merged by Google's SDK. No broad storage permission is requested.

Use Disable process to stop monitoring. Delete named routes individually or all routes in Settings. Delete trip history through Settings. Clear app storage/uninstall to remove app-private preferences, history and cached entitlement; this does not cancel/refund a Google Play purchase or delete already exported files. SpeedAlert has no app-owned sign-in account. Clearing app data does not delete purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat. Contact support about RevenueCat records associated with the anonymous app-user identifier; retention/deletion is subject to applicable legal obligations and provider policies. Google Play controls purchase/refund history and your purchasing account.

Limitations and contact

GPS and sampling can miss motion or speed changes. Background GPS/CPU use consumes battery. Configure and purchase only while parked; Google Play's external payment screen is controlled by Google. Saved limits are personal preferences, not a verified legal road-limit database. We may update this policy when the app/services change. For privacy/support contact FARHATSHINWARI@GMAIL.COM.
