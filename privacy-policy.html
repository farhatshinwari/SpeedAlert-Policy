On-device operation

SpeedAlert for Android measures GPS driving speed and provides alarms above a selected speed limit. This version has no account system, advertising, analytics or route-upload service. The app does not send location, routes, settings or selected audio files to IRYSCO LABS or third parties.

Location and background monitoring

With your permission, SpeedAlert uses precise location to estimate speed. If you enable background monitoring, location may be accessed while the app is closed or not in use, including with the screen locked. A visible foreground-service notification identifies active monitoring. Optional All-the-time location permission supports restarting a previously enabled process after reboot, subject to Android restrictions. You can disable the process, turn off background monitoring or revoke permission in Android settings. Periodic monitoring closes this app's GPS request between acquisition windows; other apps or Android may still access location.

Named routes

Only when you choose Learn route does the app store precise route sections, a route name and your selected alarm limit privately on this device. Alarms remain enabled while recording. These records are not uploaded and are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. You can edit route names and limits or delete all recorded routes in Settings. Route data remains until deleted, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Settings and sounds

Speed units, selected limits, offsets, monitoring choices and alarm selections are stored locally. When you choose an audio file, Android grants local access so the app can play it. The app does not upload it. Replacing a selected file releases the previous persistent file access where applicable. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private settings and permissions; the original audio file in your storage is not deleted by the app.

Control and deletion

Use Disable process to stop monitoring. Delete routes in Settings to erase recordings. Use Android's Clear storage or uninstall to remove all app-private route and preference data. There are no remote accounts or server records to delete.

Permissions and operation

Location permissions support speed monitoring; notifications show the ongoing process and restart reminders. A CPU wake lock supports screen-off monitoring while the process is enabled, vibration accompanies alarms, and boot reception supports optional restart. No broad storage permission is requested. Background GPS/CPU use consumes battery. GPS accuracy, audio routing and operating-system restrictions can affect alarms. Saved limits are personal choices, not verified legal road limits.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy if the app changes. Contact FARHATSHINWARI@GMAIL.COM with privacy or support questions.
